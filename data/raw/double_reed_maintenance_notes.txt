Double-Reed Maintenance Notes

Oboes should be swabbed after each rehearsal to remove moisture.
English horns should be checked regularly for loose keywork and pad leaks.
Bassoons require careful cleaning of the boot joint and tone holes.
Contrabassoons should be stored securely because of their size and weight.
Reeds should be inspected before use and replaced when damaged or unstable.
Routine maintenance can prevent larger repair problems.
Instrument cases should remain dry and free of debris.
Any mechanical problem should be reported before the instrument is used again.
